INCIDENT DESCRIPTION
A burst pipe in the second-floor bathroom caused extensive water damage to the
property. The pipe failure occurred in the supply line to the bathroom sink while
the homeowner was away at work. Water flowed for approximately 6 hours before
being discovered by a neighbor who noticed water seeping from the front door.
The water damage affected the second-floor bathroom, hallway, and the first-floor
living room and kitchen directly below.
